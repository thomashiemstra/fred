Instructions:

  1. Tape the board down and put its position in src/block_stacking/stacking_config.yaml.
  2. Camera on: the picture opens in its own window.
  3. Look: shows the blocks and the plan, nothing moves.
  4. Check tip: with the torque off, hold the gripper on a block and compare.
  5. Rehearse: the arm moves above every block and above the tower, without going down or gripping.
  6. Stack.
  7. Or switch on Click to stack: the arm waits, and puts the block you click in the camera window on the tower.

Stop lays the arm down on the table.
